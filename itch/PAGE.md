## Description (paste this)

**Ledger is like Robinhood for kids: learn how the stock market works with $1,000 of pretend money.**

Buy and sell 73 real companies in a simulated market, watch your portfolio grow (or shrink!), and learn why prices move. No real money, no ads, no chat, nothing to buy.

**What's inside**
- 📈 **Trade**: a live, simulated market with charts, dividends, price alerts and stop-losses
- 🎓 **Ledger Academy**: 35 bite-sized lessons with quizzes, from budgeting to "why do we panic-sell?"
- 🕵️ **Scam Detective**: 60 real-world scam messages. Can you spot the fake investment tip?
- ⚔️ **Bot Battles**: 60-second trading duels against 6 rival bots
- 🏆 Levels, streaks, chests, and an avatar you design yourself
- 👪 **For parents**: a Parent PIN, daily time limits, and a Parent View showing what your kid has learned. Everything is saved only on your computer.

**How to open it**
- **Mac** (Apple Silicon, M1 or newer): unzip, then **right-click Ledger → Open → Open** the first time. macOS asks because the app isn't from the App Store.
- **Windows**: unzip the folder, open **Ledger.exe**. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.
